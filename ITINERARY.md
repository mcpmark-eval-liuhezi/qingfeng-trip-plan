## 2. Travel and reimbursement policy (page 2 of the invitation)

1. Only high-speed rail (G trains) booked through 12306 is reimbursable, **second class only**.
2. Outbound: depart Beijing on Thursday, 27 August 2026, **no earlier than 07:00**, arriving in Shanghai **before 19:00**.
3. Return: depart Shanghai on Friday, 28 August 2026, on any G train **leaving after 15:00**.
4. If no direct G train fits a time window, a one-transfer itinerary found via 12306 is acceptable.

**Result:** direct G trains fit both windows, so no one-transfer itinerary is needed for either leg.

- Book **second-class (二等座) G-train tickets through 12306 only**. Any other class or train type is not reimbursable.
- Total second-class fare for the recommended pair: **CNY 667 + CNY 661 = CNY 1,328**.
- Book both legs as soon as the travel dates enter the 12306 booking window; G5/G26 class trains sell out first.
- Keep the 12306 booking confirmation and e-tickets for the reimbursement claim.
